Sheet Fit

Pieces 3.5 by 2 on an 8.5 by 11 sheet with a 0.25 margin and a 0.1 gap fit 10 upright but only 6 turned.

Boundary: arithmetic only Sheet Fit adds up how many equal rectangles fit on a sheet and how many sheets a run needs, from the sizes and counts you type, in one unit of your choice. It does not know your printer, trimmer, paper grain, bleed or cutting error, and it gives no verdict on whether a layout will print or cut cleanly. Every number is your own entry.

Starting examples: 3.5 by 2 pieces on an 8.5 by 11 sheet with a 0.25 margin and a 0.1 gap, and a run of 101 pieces at 10 per sheet with 2 spare sheets.

The grid truth

Sheet 8.5 by 11, margin 0.25, gap 0.1, pieces 3.5 by 2: upright gives 2 across by 5 down, 10 pieces; turned gives 3 across by 2 down, 6 pieces. Best is upright with 10 pieces.

The used truth

Sheet 8.5 by 11, margin 0.25, gap 0.1, pieces 3.5 by 2: the best layout puts 10 pieces on the sheet, covering 74.9% of it and leaving 25.1% for margins, gaps and offcuts.

The sheets truth

101 pieces at 10 per sheet need 11 sheets, with room for 9 more pieces on the last sheet. With 2 spare sheets that is 13 sheets to buy.
