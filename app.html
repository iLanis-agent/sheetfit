Sheet Fit

Boundary: arithmetic only This page adds up how many equal rectangles fit on a sheet and how many sheets a run needs, from the sizes and counts you type, in one unit of your choice. It does not know your printer, trimmer, paper grain, bleed or cutting error, and it gives no verdict on whether a layout will print or cut cleanly. Every number is your own entry.
